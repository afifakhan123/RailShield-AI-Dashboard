# RailShield AI

### Preventive Safety Intelligence for Women and Girls at Indian Railway Stations

**RailShield AI** is a women-centric railway safety initiative designed to help women and girls identify potential risks, navigate railway stations more safely, and access emergency assistance when they need it.

Our mission is to make railway journeys safer and help women travel with greater confidence through preventive safety intelligence.

## The Problem

For many women and girls, travelling through railway stations can involve safety concerns, uncertainty, and the fear of harassment or unsafe situations.

Some of the challenges they may face include:

- **Harassment and unwanted attention:** Concerns about staring, following, verbal harassment, or inappropriate behaviour in crowded station areas.
- **Isolated and poorly lit areas:** Footbridges, underpasses, stairways, waiting areas, and less crowded sections can feel unsafe, especially at night.
- **Difficulty identifying safer routes:** The shortest path to a platform may pass through areas with limited visibility, fewer people, or less access to assistance.
- **Overcrowding and unwanted physical contact:** Dense crowds can make women feel vulnerable and make it difficult to move away from uncomfortable or threatening situations.
- **Limited awareness of nearby assistance:** Passengers may not know where railway police, security personnel, help desks, or emergency facilities are located.
- **Fear of travelling alone at night:** Women travelling alone may have additional concerns about reaching platforms, waiting for trains, or navigating unfamiliar stations.
- **Difficulty getting help quickly:** During an emergency, a passenger may struggle to communicate their location or contact trusted people promptly.
- **Sudden platform changes and confusion:** Last-minute changes can force passengers to move quickly through unfamiliar or crowded areas.


## Our Solution

RailShield AI aims to provide a preventive safety platform specifically designed for women and girls travelling through Indian railway stations.

Rather than focusing only on navigation, it aims to assess available safety information, highlight potentially higher-risk areas, recommend routes that prioritize safety, and make emergency assistance easier to access.

## Key Features

### 1. Station Risk Score

Displays a station-level safety indicator based on available information, such as lighting, crowd conditions, CCTV coverage, and access to assistance.

### 2. SafeZone Heatmap

Displays station areas using green, yellow, and red indicators to represent different assessed risk levels.

### 3. Women's Safe Route

Recommends routes based on safety-related factors, including lighting, CCTV coverage, crowd presence, and proximity to staffed help points, rather than distance alone.

### 4. Crowd Prediction

Identifies or predicts crowded areas so women can make more informed decisions when navigating platforms and station facilities.

### 5. Platform Change Alerts

Helps passengers respond to platform changes with clear guidance and safer-route recommendations.

### 6. Unsafe Area Alerts

Provides warnings when a passenger approaches an area identified by the system as potentially higher risk.

### 7. Night Safety Mode

Prioritizes routes with better visibility, populated areas, monitoring, and access to assistance when travelling at night.

### 8. Trusted Companion and Safety Check-In

Allows users to share their journey or location with a trusted contact and receive check-in reminders.

### 9. Emergency SOS

Provides a prominent emergency interface designed to help users initiate an alert, share their location, notify trusted contacts, and access available emergency services.

### 10. Voice and Shake SOS

Explores hands-free emergency activation through a voice command or phone-shake gesture.

### 11. Railway Safety Dashboard

A proposed dashboard for authorized railway safety personnel to view station risk indicators, crowd conditions, safety incidents, and active emergency alerts.

## How It Works

1. The user opens RailShield AI and enters journey details.
2. The application identifies the relevant station using location permission or manual station selection.
3. Available station information is evaluated using defined safety factors.
4. The system displays a station risk indicator and color-coded safety zones.
5. The user views safety-oriented route recommendations and relevant alerts.
6. If assistance is needed, the user can access the emergency interface and available contact options.

RailShield AI is under development.
